Employee Exit and Offboarding Process

1. Resignation Notice
Upon receiving a resignation, managers should notify HR within 24 hours to begin the
offboarding process. The standard notice period (per the Leave Policy) applies unless
otherwise negotiated.

2. Knowledge Transfer
Departing employees are expected to document key processes and hand off active projects
to a designated successor or their manager during the notice period. A knowledge transfer
checklist is provided by HR at the start of the notice period.

3. Exit Interview
HR conducts a confidential exit interview with every departing employee during their final
week, covering reasons for leaving and feedback on the employee experience. Aggregated,
anonymized themes are shared with leadership quarterly.

4. Final Pay and Benefits
Final paycheck, including any unused accrued leave payout where applicable by law, is
processed on the employee's last working day or the next standard payroll cycle,
depending on local regulations. COBRA or equivalent benefits continuation information is
provided within 14 days of departure.

5. Access Revocation
All system access, including email, VPN, and internal tools, is automatically revoked at
11:59 PM on the employee's last working day. Company equipment must be returned per the
IT Equipment and Asset Policy.
