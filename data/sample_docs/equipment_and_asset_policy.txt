IT Equipment and Asset Policy

1. Equipment Issuance
New employees are issued a standard laptop, monitor, keyboard, and mouse upon joining.
Additional equipment requests (e.g., a second monitor, ergonomic chair) require manager
approval and are fulfilled through the IT asset request portal.

2. Equipment Care
Employees are responsible for the reasonable care of all company-issued equipment.
Accidental damage is generally covered, but repeated loss or damage due to negligence may
result in the cost being deducted from reimbursement allowances.

3. Software Installation
Only software approved on the internal software catalog may be installed on company
devices. Requests for new software must go through IT Security review before installation,
particularly for tools that access or store company data.

4. Asset Return
All company-issued equipment must be returned to IT within 5 business days of an
employee's last working day. Unreturned equipment will be invoiced to the employee at
replacement cost.

5. Lost or Stolen Equipment
Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT Security immediately, and to local law
enforcement if theft is suspected. Devices are remotely wiped as soon as a loss is
reported to protect company data.
