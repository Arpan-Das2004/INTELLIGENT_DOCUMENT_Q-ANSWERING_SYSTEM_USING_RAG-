Data Privacy Policy

1. Data Classification
Company data is classified into three tiers: Public, Internal, and Restricted. Restricted
data includes customer personal information, financial records, and employee PII, and
requires the highest level of access control and encryption.

2. Access Control
Access to Restricted data is granted on a need-to-know basis and must be approved by the
relevant data owner. Access is reviewed quarterly, and unused access is automatically
revoked after 90 days of inactivity.

3. Customer Data Handling
Customer personal data must never be exported to personal devices or non-approved third
party tools. Any data processing agreement with a vendor must be reviewed by Legal before
customer data can be shared externally.

4. Data Retention
Customer data is retained only as long as necessary to provide the service or as required
by law, typically no more than 7 years after account closure. Automated deletion jobs run
quarterly to purge expired records.

5. Breach Notification
Any suspected data breach must be reported to the Data Protection Officer within 1 hour
of discovery. Depending on severity and jurisdiction, affected customers and regulators
may need to be notified within 72 hours, in line with applicable privacy law.
