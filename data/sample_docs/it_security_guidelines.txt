IT Security Guidelines

1. Password Policy
All employees must use passwords that are at least 12 characters long, containing a mix of
uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters. Passwords must be
changed every 90 days and cannot be reused from the last 5 passwords.

2. Two-Factor Authentication
Two-factor authentication (2FA) is mandatory for all accounts accessing company systems,
including email, VPN, and internal tools. Employees must enroll in 2FA within 48 hours of
receiving system access.

3. Device Usage
Only company-approved devices may be used to access internal systems. Personal devices
must be enrolled in the Mobile Device Management (MDM) system before accessing company
email or files. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT Security within 1 hour of
discovery.

4. Data Handling
Confidential company data must not be stored on personal cloud storage services such as
personal Google Drive or Dropbox accounts. All confidential files must be stored only in
approved company systems. Data classified as "Restricted" requires encryption both at rest
and in transit.

5. Incident Reporting
Any suspected security incident, including phishing emails or unauthorized access attempts,
must be reported to the IT Security team immediately via the internal incident portal or by
emailing security@company.com. Response time for critical incidents is within 30 minutes
during business hours.
