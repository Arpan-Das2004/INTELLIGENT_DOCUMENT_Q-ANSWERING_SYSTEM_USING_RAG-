Workplace Health and Safety Policy

1. General Responsibility
The company is committed to providing a safe working environment for all employees,
contractors, and visitors. Employees are expected to follow posted safety procedures and
report any hazards immediately to Facilities or their manager.

2. Emergency Procedures
Evacuation routes are posted on every floor near elevators and stairwells. Fire drills are
conducted twice a year. In the event of an emergency, employees should proceed to the
designated assembly point and await an all-clear from the floor warden.

3. Ergonomics
Employees experiencing discomfort from their workstation setup can request an ergonomic
assessment through Facilities at no cost. Standing desks and ergonomic chairs are available
upon request with manager approval.

4. Injury Reporting
Any workplace injury, however minor, must be reported to HR and Facilities within 24
hours using the incident report form. This is required both for employee wellbeing and
for workers' compensation compliance.

5. Mental Health Support
Employees have access to a confidential Employee Assistance Program (EAP) offering free
counseling sessions, available 24/7. Managers are trained to recognize signs of burnout
and are encouraged to proactively check in with their teams.
