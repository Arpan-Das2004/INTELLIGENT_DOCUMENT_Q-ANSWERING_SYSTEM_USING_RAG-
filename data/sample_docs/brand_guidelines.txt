Marketing Brand Guidelines

1. Logo Usage
The primary logo must always be used on a white or light background with a minimum
clear-space margin equal to the height of the logomark. The logo should never be
stretched, recolored outside the approved palette, or placed on busy background images.

2. Color Palette
The primary brand color is Deep Indigo (#2C2A4A). Secondary colors are Coral (#FF6B6B) and
Soft Teal (#4ECDC4), used for accents and calls-to-action. Text should default to Charcoal
(#2D2D2D) on light backgrounds for accessibility compliance.

3. Typography
Headlines use the "Satoshi" typeface in Bold or Medium weight. Body copy uses "Inter"
Regular at a minimum of 16px for web content to meet accessibility guidelines. Avoid using
more than two typefaces in a single piece of marketing material.

4. Tone of Voice
Brand communications should be clear, confident, and approachable — avoiding corporate
jargon and overly technical language unless writing for a developer audience. Humor is
welcome in social media content but should be avoided in compliance or legal
communications.

5. Asset Approval
All external-facing marketing materials (ads, landing pages, press materials) must be
reviewed and approved by the Brand team before publication. Internal presentation
templates are exempt from this review requirement.
