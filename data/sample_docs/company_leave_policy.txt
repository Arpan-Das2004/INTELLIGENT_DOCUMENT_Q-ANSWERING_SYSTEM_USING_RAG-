Company Leave Policy

1. Annual Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave accrues monthly at a rate of 1.5 days per month. Unused annual leave can be carried
forward to the next year up to a maximum of 10 days. Any leave beyond this cap will be
forfeited unless prior written approval is obtained from the HR department.

2. Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is
required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be carried forward
and does not convert to cash upon resignation.

3. Maternity and Paternity Leave
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Male employees are
entitled to 2 weeks of paid paternity leave, to be availed within 3 months of the child's birth.

4. Work From Home Policy
Employees may request to work from home up to 2 days per week, subject to manager
approval. Requests should be submitted at least 24 hours in advance through the HR portal.

5. Resignation and Notice Period
The standard notice period for resignation is 60 days for full-time employees and 30 days
for employees who have been with the company for less than 1 year. Notice period may be
bought out at the discretion of the reporting manager and HR.
