Expense Reimbursement Policy

1. Eligible Expenses
Employees may claim reimbursement for business travel, client meals, approved software
subscriptions, and conference registration fees. Personal expenses, alcohol (except at
approved client events), and first-class travel upgrades are not reimbursable.

2. Submission Process
All expense claims must be submitted through the finance portal within 30 days of the
expense being incurred. Claims submitted after 60 days will not be processed without
VP-level approval. Each claim requires an itemized receipt; credit card statements alone
are not sufficient proof of purchase.

3. Approval Limits
Expenses under $100 are auto-approved upon manager sign-off. Expenses between $100 and
$1,000 require manager and department head approval. Any expense above $1,000 requires
additional approval from the Finance team before reimbursement is processed.

4. Travel Expenses
Domestic flights must be booked at least 14 days in advance through the approved travel
portal to qualify for reimbursement. Economy class is standard; business class is only
approved for flights longer than 6 hours with VP approval. Mileage for personal vehicle
use is reimbursed at the current IRS standard rate.

5. Reimbursement Timeline
Approved claims are reimbursed within 10 business days via direct deposit, included in
the next regular payroll cycle.
