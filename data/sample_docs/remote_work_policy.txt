Remote Work Policy

1. Eligibility
Remote work arrangements are available to employees whose roles do not require regular
on-site presence, subject to manager approval. Eligibility is reviewed annually or upon
role change.

2. Hybrid Schedule
Employees on a hybrid schedule are expected to be in the office at least 2 days per week,
typically Tuesday and Thursday, to support team collaboration. Core collaboration hours
are 10 AM to 4 PM local time, during which employees should be reachable regardless of
location.

3. Fully Remote Employees
Fully remote employees must reside in a state or country where the company is legally
registered to employ workers. A one-time home office setup stipend of $500 is provided,
along with a monthly $50 internet/utilities allowance.

4. Equipment and Security
Remote employees must use company-issued laptops with full-disk encryption and connect
to internal systems only via the company VPN. Public Wi-Fi networks should not be used to
access confidential company data without VPN protection.

5. Performance Expectations
Remote work arrangements do not change performance expectations. Managers may request a
return to in-office work if performance or collaboration issues arise that cannot be
resolved remotely.
