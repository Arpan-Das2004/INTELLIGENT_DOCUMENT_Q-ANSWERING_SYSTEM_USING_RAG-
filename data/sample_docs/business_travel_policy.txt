Business Travel Policy

1. Trip Approval
All business travel must be pre-approved by the employee's manager at least 7 days before
departure for domestic trips, and 21 days before departure for international trips.
Emergency travel may be approved on shorter notice with VP sign-off.

2. Booking Process
All flights, hotels, and rental cars must be booked through the company's approved travel
platform to ensure duty-of-care tracking and negotiated corporate rates. Bookings made
outside this platform will not be reimbursed except in documented emergencies.

3. Accommodation Guidelines
Hotel bookings should not exceed $250 per night in standard cities, or $350 per night in
high-cost cities such as New York, London, or Tokyo, unless no reasonably priced
alternative is available near the business venue.

4. International Travel
Employees traveling internationally for business must complete a pre-travel safety
briefing and ensure their passport is valid for at least 6 months beyond the travel date.
A company-provided travel insurance policy automatically covers all approved international
trips.

5. Per Diem
A daily meal per diem of $75 domestic / $100 international is provided for trips without
pre-arranged catering. Per diem does not require itemized receipts but must be logged in
the expense system.
