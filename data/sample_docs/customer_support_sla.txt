Customer Support SLA

1. Response Time Targets
Priority 1 (system down) tickets receive a first response within 1 hour, 24/7. Priority 2
(major functionality impaired) tickets receive a first response within 4 business hours.
Priority 3 (minor issue, question) tickets receive a first response within 1 business day.

2. Resolution Time Targets
Priority 1 issues target resolution within 8 hours. Priority 2 issues target resolution
within 3 business days. Priority 3 issues target resolution within 5 business days,
though complex issues may require a documented extension with customer notification.

3. Escalation Path
Tickets unresolved within 150% of their target resolution time are automatically escalated
to a team lead. Enterprise customers have a dedicated escalation contact who can be
reached directly for Priority 1 issues.

4. Support Channels
Support is available via email, in-app chat, and phone (Enterprise plan only). Phone
support operates Monday-Friday, 8 AM - 8 PM customer local time. Email and chat support
operate 24/7 for Priority 1 issues.

5. Customer Satisfaction
Every resolved ticket triggers a CSAT survey. The support team targets a CSAT score of
90% or higher, reviewed monthly, with coaching provided for agents below target.
