Vendor Management Policy

1. Vendor Onboarding
New vendors must complete a security questionnaire and, for vendors handling company or
customer data, a data processing agreement (DPA) before any contract can be signed.
Vendor onboarding is coordinated by Procurement in partnership with Legal and IT Security.

2. Contract Approval Thresholds
Vendor contracts under $10,000 annually can be approved by a department head. Contracts
between $10,000 and $100,000 require VP approval. Contracts exceeding $100,000 require
CFO and Legal sign-off.

3. Vendor Risk Tiering
Vendors are tiered by risk: Tier 1 (access to Restricted data or core systems), Tier 2
(access to Internal data), and Tier 3 (no data access, e.g. office supplies). Tier 1
vendors undergo an annual security review; Tier 2 vendors are reviewed every two years.

4. Performance Reviews
Vendors providing ongoing services (e.g., cloud infrastructure, outsourced support) are
formally reviewed annually against agreed SLAs. Underperforming vendors are placed on a
90-day improvement plan before contract renewal is considered.

5. Vendor Offboarding
When a vendor relationship ends, all access credentials must be revoked within 24 hours
of contract termination, and any company data held by the vendor must be returned or
certified as destroyed within 30 days.
