Employee Code of Conduct

1. Professional Behavior
All employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect and
professionalism. Harassment, discrimination, or retaliation of any kind will not be
tolerated and may result in immediate termination.

2. Conflicts of Interest
Employees must disclose any financial interest, familial relationship, or outside
employment that could reasonably create a conflict of interest with their role. Disclosures
should be made to HR via the annual conflict-of-interest form or as situations arise.

3. Confidentiality
Employees must not disclose confidential company information, trade secrets, or
non-public financial data to any party outside the organization, both during employment
and after departure, as outlined in the signed confidentiality agreement.

4. Gifts and Entertainment
Employees may accept gifts from vendors or clients valued under $75. Any gift exceeding
this amount must be reported to the employee's manager and may need to be declined or
returned, depending on company guidelines.

5. Reporting Violations
Employees who witness a violation of this code of conduct should report it to HR, their
manager, or through the anonymous ethics hotline. Retaliation against anyone making a
good-faith report is strictly prohibited.
