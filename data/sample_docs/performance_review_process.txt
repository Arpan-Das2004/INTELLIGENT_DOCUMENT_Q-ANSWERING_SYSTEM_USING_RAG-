Performance Review Process

1. Review Cycle
Formal performance reviews are conducted twice a year: a mid-year check-in in June and
an annual review in December. Both cycles involve a self-assessment, manager assessment,
and optional peer feedback.

2. Rating Scale
Employees are rated on a 5-point scale: Exceeds Expectations, Strong Performance, Meets
Expectations, Needs Improvement, and Unsatisfactory. Ratings are calibrated across teams
by department heads to ensure consistency before being finalized.

3. Goal Setting
At the start of each cycle, employees and managers jointly set 3-5 measurable goals using
the OKR (Objectives and Key Results) framework. Progress against these goals is reviewed
at the mid-year check-in and finalized at the annual review.

4. Promotion Consideration
Promotion decisions are made once a year, following the annual review cycle. Employees
being considered for promotion require a "Strong Performance" or higher rating in the two
most recent cycles, along with a promotion packet submitted by their manager.

5. Performance Improvement Plans
Employees rated "Needs Improvement" or "Unsatisfactory" are placed on a formal Performance
Improvement Plan (PIP) with specific, time-bound goals, typically lasting 30-60 days, with
regular check-ins with their manager and an HR business partner.
