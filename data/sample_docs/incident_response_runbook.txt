Incident Response Runbook

1. Severity Levels
Incidents are classified as SEV1 (full outage, critical data loss), SEV2 (major feature
broken, significant customer impact), or SEV3 (minor issue, limited impact). Severity
determines response time and escalation path.

2. Response Times
SEV1 incidents require acknowledgment within 5 minutes and an incident commander assigned
within 10 minutes. SEV2 incidents require acknowledgment within 30 minutes. SEV3 incidents
are addressed during normal business hours.

3. Incident Commander Role
The Incident Commander (IC) coordinates the response, makes key decisions, and is the
single point of communication to stakeholders during an incident. The IC does not
necessarily perform the technical fix themselves.

4. Communication
A status update must be posted to the incident channel every 30 minutes during an active
SEV1 or SEV2 incident, even if there is no new information, to keep stakeholders informed.
Customer-facing status page updates are the responsibility of the IC or their delegate.

5. Postmortems
Every SEV1 and SEV2 incident requires a blameless postmortem document within 5 business
days of resolution, covering root cause, timeline, impact, and concrete action items with
owners and due dates.
