New Employee Onboarding Guide

1. Before Your First Day
New hires receive a welcome email 5 business days before their start date, containing
laptop setup instructions, building access details, and a link to the online onboarding
portal. IT provisions all accounts (email, Slack, internal tools) 48 hours before start date.

2. First Day Checklist
Report to the front desk by 9:30 AM to collect your access badge. Your manager or an
assigned buddy will walk you through desk setup, team introductions, and a tour of the
office. HR will conduct a 1-hour orientation session covering benefits enrollment, payroll
setup, and company policies.

3. First Week Goals
New employees should complete all mandatory compliance training (security awareness, code
of conduct, anti-harassment) within the first 5 working days. Managers are expected to hold
a 1:1 check-in by the end of week one to review role expectations and initial goals.

4. 30-60-90 Day Plan
Each new hire works with their manager to set concrete goals for the first 30, 60, and 90
days. A formal 90-day review is conducted to assess onboarding effectiveness and confirm
successful completion of the probationary period.

5. Buddy Program
Every new employee is paired with a buddy from a different team for their first 90 days,
to help with informal questions and cross-team networking. Buddy assignments are managed
by the People Operations team.
