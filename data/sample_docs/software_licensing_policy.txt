Software Licensing Policy

1. Approved Software List
Employees may only install software that appears on the company's approved software
catalog, maintained by IT. Requests for new tools must be submitted through the IT portal
and reviewed for security, licensing cost, and data handling implications.

2. License Tracking
All paid software licenses are tracked centrally by IT to avoid duplicate purchases and
ensure compliance with per-seat licensing terms. Department heads receive a quarterly
report of active licenses and utilization for their team.

3. Open Source Usage
Use of open source libraries in company products must comply with the company's approved
license list (MIT, Apache 2.0, BSD are generally pre-approved). Libraries under GPL or
other copyleft licenses require Legal review before use in shipped products.

4. Unused License Reclamation
Software licenses unused for 60 consecutive days are automatically flagged for reclamation
and reassignment to another employee, to control software spend. Employees are notified
before a license is reclaimed.

5. Personal Use Restriction
Company-purchased software licenses are for business use only and must not be used for
personal projects or shared with individuals outside the company, in line with vendor
licensing terms.
