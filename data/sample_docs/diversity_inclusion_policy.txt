Diversity and Inclusion Policy

1. Commitment Statement
The company is committed to building a workforce that reflects a wide range of
backgrounds, perspectives, and experiences, and to fostering a workplace where every
employee feels respected and able to do their best work.

2. Inclusive Hiring Practices
All hiring panels for roles above entry-level must include at least one interviewer from
outside the immediate hiring team to reduce bias. Structured interview scorecards with
consistent criteria are required for all candidates for a given role.

3. Pay Equity
An annual pay equity audit is conducted across all departments to identify and address
unexplained pay gaps based on gender, race, or other protected characteristics. Results
are reviewed by the Compensation Committee and corrective adjustments are budgeted
accordingly.

4. Employee Resource Groups
The company supports several Employee Resource Groups (ERGs), each with a modest annual
budget for events and initiatives. New ERGs can be formed with a minimum of 10 interested
employees and a sponsoring executive.

5. Training
All employees complete unconscious bias training within their first 90 days, with a
refresher course required every 2 years. Managers complete additional inclusive leadership
training as part of their promotion into a people-management role.
