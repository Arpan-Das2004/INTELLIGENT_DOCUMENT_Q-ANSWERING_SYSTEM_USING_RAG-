Meeting Culture Guidelines

1. Default Meeting Length
Meetings should default to 25 or 50 minutes instead of 30 or 60, to leave buffer time
between back-to-back meetings. Calendar settings company-wide are configured with this
default.

2. Agenda Requirement
Any meeting with more than 3 attendees requires a written agenda shared at least 24 hours
in advance. Meetings without a clear agenda or objective may be declined by invitees.

3. No-Meeting Blocks
Wednesday afternoons (1 PM - 5 PM) are designated as company-wide no-meeting time to
allow for focused, uninterrupted work. Exceptions are limited to urgent, time-sensitive
issues.

4. Meeting Size
Decision-making meetings should include no more than 8 participants where possible.
Larger informational meetings are acceptable but should be structured as a presentation
with a dedicated Q&A segment rather than open discussion.

5. Notes and Follow-up
Every meeting should have a designated note-taker who shares a summary with action items
and owners within 24 hours. Recurring meetings without documented follow-up actions for
3 consecutive sessions should be reevaluated for necessity.
